# Policy brief for the Director, Disease Control & Preparedness Unit

## EpiScope: act earlier, allocate fairly

EpiScope is a prototype decision support system for dengue, diarrhoeal disease, influenza-like illness and malaria. It combines routine facility reports, weather signals and facility stock snapshots. Every person-level health record in the prototype is synthetic; no personal information is shown to dashboard users.

### Current situation

Seasonal disease demand can rise faster than district stocks and beds can be replenished. Routine reports also arrive late and sometimes contain errors. EpiScope separates unmodified incoming reports from checked, de-identified records, so decision-makers can see both the warning signal and the quality of the evidence behind it.

### Three recommended actions

1. Use district-week warning signals as a trigger for verification, not an automatic declaration of an outbreak. This helps act earlier while avoiding unnecessary alert fatigue.
2. Pre-position IV saline, rapid tests and oral rehydration supplies where forecast demand exceeds current stock. The allocation view makes the trade-off between severity and a minimum equitable share visible.
3. Begin prevention campaigns before the locally high-risk seasonal weeks in the preparedness calendar, then adjust the response as observed reports arrive.

### How to read the dashboard

Select a district or disease once; all charts update together. The time series distinguishes observed reports, forecasts, alert thresholds and uncertainty. The ranked table shows the highest current warnings. The allocation view compares forecast demand, stock and the recommended response. Counts between one and four are shown as “Suppressed”; this is a privacy safeguard, not zero cases.

### Confidence, limits and data protection

Forecasts are uncertain and should support, never replace, clinical and epidemiological judgement. Late reporting, changes in testing and silent facilities may create misleading signals. The system records data-quality results for every load and retains source lineage for audit. Direct identifiers stop at the protected bronze layer, the dashboard receives only aggregated records, and small cells are suppressed. Before operational use, the Ministry should independently validate performance on representative routine data, agree escalation protocols with districts, and establish role-based access and audit review.
